Property investment · Practical guide

Five questions to ask before investing in property.

A considered investment decision begins with understanding the asset, the numbers and your own objectives.

Property can play different roles in an investment plan. Before committing capital, take time to understand what you are buying, why it may suit your goals and what could affect its performance.

1. What is the purpose of this investment?

Be clear whether your priority is rental income, long-term appreciation, business use, diversification or another objective.

2. What do I know about the location?

Consider access, surrounding land uses, infrastructure and local demand. Verify claims independently.

3. What are the full costs?

Look beyond the purchase price. Understand taxes, legal fees, financing, service charges, maintenance and vacancy periods.

4. What risks should I plan for?

Consider title due diligence, construction or condition risks, vacancy, liquidity and market changes. Seek qualified advice.

5. Does it fit my time horizon?

Property is not always easy to sell quickly. Match the investment to your capital needs and tolerance for uncertainty.
